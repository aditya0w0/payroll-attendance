## Executive Summary

All 12 findings from `anti-slop/audit-003-2026-11-18.md` addressing **"too much texto / visual clutter"** have been resolved across the employee directory, attendance roster, shifts, and payroll views.

Key improvements:
1. **Sleek Employee Directory (`/owner/employees`)**:
   - Replaced verbose AI subtitle padding with a clean badge counter (`{employees.length} terdaftar`).
   - Added instant search and status filtering (`Cari nama, ID karyawan, atau shift...` + status selector).
   - Eliminated duplicate time string stacking in the shift column (`Shift Malam` + `16:00 – 00:00 WIB`).
   - Streamlined action controls from clunky dual colored buttons to a compact `Atur Gaji` button and an unobtrusive reset icon button.
   - Stripped redundant explanatory paragraphs from modals.
2. **Decluttered Attendance Roster (`src/components/owner/EmployeeAttendanceRoster.tsx`)**:
   - Removed secondary microcopy lines (`dari 6 shift`, `Shift dilewati`, `Tarif: Rp.../hari`).
   - Eliminated badge pill overload in every column; replaced with clean tabular figures with subtle semantic indicator dots (`0 / 6`, `1x (15m)`, `0 hari`).
3. **Refined Shift & Payroll Views (`/owner/shifts`, `/owner/payroll`)**:
   - Simplified shift card microcopy (`Toleransi: 10 menit`, `Denda: ...`, `Staf: ...`).
   - Removed tutorial-style filler subtitles beneath KPI monetary cards.
   - Cleaned table headers to concise titles (`Hari Kerja`, `Terlambat`, `Total Menit`).
4. **Header Polish (`/owner/devices`, `/owner/audit-logs`, `/owner/attendance`)**:
   - Replaced verbose multi-sentence paragraphs with minimal header hierarchy.
