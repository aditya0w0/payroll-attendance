# Anti-Slop Audit Report #002

Date: 2026-11-18
Target: Payroll & QR Attendance Platform (CyberGuard)
Audit Scope: Full Application Copywriting, Typography, Terminology, Language Consistency, and UX Microcopy
Standard: Anti Slop Rules for AI Coding Agents (R-01 to R-40) & antislop-copywriting

---

## Executive Summary

Following manual inspection of the running platform, a deep full audit of application copywriting and microcopy was conducted across all admin consoles (`/owner/*`), employee views (`/employee/*`), and dialog overlays.

The audit revealed severe AI copywriting patterns:
1. **Machine Token Brackets**: Diagnostic bracket dumps like `[ON-TIME]`, `[LATE]`, and `[ALPA]` displayed as UI content.
2. **Robotic AI Padding**: Formulaic subtitle templates ("Configure X, assign Y, and manage Z", "Aggregation of X minus Y").
3. **Bilingual Fragmentation (Indolish)**: English page titles paired with Indonesian subtitles, mixed with Indonesian currency (IDR) and business timezone (WIB).
4. **Programmatic Enum Exposure**: Raw backend code constants (`PER_MINUTE`, `PER_HOUR`, `ROUND_UP`, `CONTRACT`, `PERMANENT`) rendered directly in data tables.
5. **Foreign Currency Symbol Collision**: Using foreign dollar signs (`$ Set Salary`) in an Indonesian Rupiah platform.
6. **Redundant Duplication & Uppercase Tracking**: Duplicating shift hours twice in the same cell and using mechanical ALL-CAPS tracking on section headings.

Below is the prioritized list of 12 actionable findings. Per antislop guidelines (Mode 2), no files will be modified until you approve the specific numbers to fix.

## Detailed Findings

### Finding 1: Machine Token Brackets in UI Cells
- **Rule**: R-02 (Copywriting: Natural Human Language) & `antislop-copywriting`
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/components/owner/EmployeeAttendanceRoster.tsx` (lines 254, 271, 292)
- **One-line reason**: Monospace machine tokens `[ON-TIME]`, `[LATE]`, and `[ALPA]` look like raw debug dumps rather than human-designed status tags.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Replace bracketed tokens with clean semantic status tags: `Tepat Waktu` (hijau), `Terlambat` (amber), and `Alpa` (rose), rendered in natural font without brackets.

### Finding 2: Foreign Dollar Symbol & Ambiguous Reset Action
- **Rule**: R-15 (Call to Action) & R-36 (No Fabricated Claims)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (lines 296, 306)
- **One-line reason**: Displaying a dollar symbol (`$ Set Salary`) in an Indonesian Rupiah platform and a generic red `Reset` button without stating what is being reset.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Replace `$ Set Salary` with `Ubah Gaji` (with wallet/coin icon or clean text), and replace `Reset` with `Reset Perangkat` with clear descriptive confirmation.

### Finding 3: Formulaic AI Padding in Page Subtitles
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting & Buzzwords) & `antislop-copywriting` (Empty AI Vocabulary)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (line 193), `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (line 141), `src/app/owner/payroll/page.tsx` (line 159), `src/app/owner/devices/page.tsx` (line 257), `src/app/owner/audit-logs/page.tsx` (line 170)
- **One-line reason**: Subtitles follow the classic 3-verb AI generation formula ("Configure employee profiles, assign daily salaries, and manage single-device authorizations").
- **Proposed Remedy**: Rewrite subtitles into concise, human Indonesian statements describing real everyday owner actions (e.g., "Daftar seluruh karyawan aktif, jadwal shift kerja, dan status pengikatan perangkat.").

### Finding 4: Programmatic Raw ENUM Strings Dumped in Tables
- **Rule**: R-38 (Real Content or Honest Placeholder) & R-02
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 225-235), `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (line 268)
- **One-line reason**: Raw database enum constants (`PER_MINUTE`, `PER_HOUR`, `ROUND_UP`, `CONTRACT`, `PERMANENT`) exposed directly to end-users without translation.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Provide human mapping: `Per Menit`, `Per Jam (Pembulatan ke Atas)`, `Karyawan Tetap`, `Kontrak`.

### Finding 5: Inconsistent Bilingual Fragmentation (Indolish)
- **Rule**: R-20 (Visual & Verbal Identity) & `antislop-copywriting`
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/layout.tsx`, `src/app/owner/*`, `src/components/owner/*`
- **One-line reason**: Titles are in English ("Attendance & Absence Management") while subtitles and tabs are in Indonesian ("Breakdown performa kehadiran...").
- **Proposed Remedy**: Unify all interface titles, navigation, and feedback into professional, polished Indonesian appropriate for an Indonesian payroll application.

### Finding 6: Mechanical All-Caps Uppercase Tracking Headings
- **Rule**: R-06 (Typography) & `antislop-copywriting` (All-Caps & Generic Typography)
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 172, 214), `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (lines 232-238)
- **One-line reason**: Forcing section headers and table titles into wide-tracked all-caps (`CONFIGURED WORK SHIFTS`, `DEDUCTION POLICIES`) is a primary visual tell of AI templates.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Convert section headers and table headers to natural Title Case or Sentence Case with standard letter spacing.

### Finding 7: Technical Specification Badges Parked Next to Titles
- **Rule**: R-09 (Badges) & R-16 (Buzzwords)
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/devices/page.tsx` (lines 252-254), `src/app/owner/audit-logs/page.tsx` (lines 165-167)
- **One-line reason**: Parked capsule pills (`FIDO2 Passkeys & W3C WebAuthn`, `SHA-256 Chain • Ed25519 Signed`) act as decorative technical boasting rather than functional status indicators.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Remove technical badges from H1 headers; describe security capabilities naturally in contextual documentation or settings info.

### Finding 8: Duplicate Schedule Hours Within Same Row
- **Rule**: R-14 (Component Craftsmanship) & R-02
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 180-184), `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (lines 260-264)
- **One-line reason**: Shift hours are rendered redundantly twice in the same cell (e.g., `Evening Shift (16:00 - 00:00)` with `16:00 - 00:00 (Overnight)` immediately underneath).
- **Proposed Remedy**: Display the shift title cleanly without repeating the exact same hour numbers in two stacked lines.

### Finding 9: Stark 0% Empty Attendance Progress Bar
- **Rule**: R-27 (UI States) & `antislop-human`
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/components/owner/EmployeeAttendanceRoster.tsx` (lines 307-320)
- **One-line reason**: Rendering an empty red 0% progress track (`0/6`) for a period that has not started creates false alarm and negative sentiment.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Display a neutral indicator (e.g. `Belum ada jadwal lewat` or `-`) when total scheduled shifts elapsed is 0.

### Finding 10: Typographical Spacing Glitch on Card Labels
- **Rule**: R-02 (Text Hygiene)
- **Tier**: Quality Lock
- **Priority**: LOW
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (line 198)
- **One-line reason**: Missing space after colon (`Deduction:Hourly Deduction`) creates an unpolished, machine-generated appearance.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Format labels cleanly as `Kebijakan Denda: [Nama Kebijakan]` with proper spacing and natural phrasing.

### Finding 11: Generic Template Action Button Copy
- **Rule**: R-15 (Call to Action)
- **Tier**: Quality Lock
- **Priority**: LOW
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 146-154), `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (lines 198-203)
- **One-line reason**: Generic `+ Add Employee`, `+ New Shift`, and `+ New Policy` labels repeat default AI template text.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Contextualize action labels to natural Indonesian: `Tambah Karyawan`, `Tambah Shift Baru`, `Buat Kebijakan Denda`.

### Finding 12: Repetitive Microcopy Clutter
- **Rule**: R-31 (Every Decision Must Have a Reason) & C-3
- **Tier**: Quality Lock
- **Priority**: LOW
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (line 256)
- **One-line reason**: The label `base daily rate` is repeated mechanically under every single row, adding visual noise since the table column already states `Daily Salary`.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Remove `base daily rate` subtitle under each row to give numbers clean tabular breathing room.
