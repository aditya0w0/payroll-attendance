## Executive Summary

Following a manual review of the Shifts & Deduction Policies page (`/owner/shifts`) and cross-referencing with the Employee Directory (`/owner/employees`) and Attendance Roster, an in-depth audit was performed targeting **AI slop writing, redundant data packing, and "too much texto"**.

The audit identified critical craftsmanship flaws where the shift management interface exhibits hallmark AI patterns:
1. **Redundant Data Packing in Policy Titles**: Deduction policies in database seeds and display tables redundantly embed the entire row's parameters inside the name string, e.g. `Per Minute Standard (Rp500/min, 10m Grace)` and `Hourly Deduction (Rp10,000/hr, Round Up, 15m Grace)`.
2. **Duplicate Time Range Echo**: Shift cards and table rows display `Morning Shift (08:00 - 17:00)` and immediately repeat `08:00 - 17:00` on the line below, doubling the reading burden.
3. **Dense Key-Value Stacking in Shift Cards**: Shift cards stack 3 full rows of verbose labels (`Toleransi: 10 menit`, `Denda: ...`, `Staf: X orang`) with long red text strings wrapping awkwardly, rather than using sleek modern metrics or chips.
4. **Tutorial-Style Modal Copywriting**: Form labels and select options use long tutorial explanations in parentheses, such as `Shift Lintas Hari (Melewati tengah malam hingga hari kalender berikutnya)` and `Bulatkan ke Atas (Jam Penuh Berikutnya)`.
5. **Table Column & Cell Bloat**: Deduction policies table contains redundant text like `Proporsional Riil` for per-minute calculations where hourly rounding does not apply, and repetitive `% upah harian` suffixes.
6. **Double Time String Cascading**: In employee modal dropdowns, shifts are rendered as `{s.name} ({s.startTime}-{s.endTime})`, causing clumsy double echoes like `Morning Shift (08:00 - 17:00) (08:00-17:00)`.
7. **Missing Interactive Action Controls**: Shift cards and deduction policy table rows are static display elements with no direct edit or delete capabilities, violating functional completeness (C-2, R-26).

Below is the prioritized list of 8 actionable findings. Per antislop guidelines (Mode 2), no code files will be modified until you approve the specific numbers to fix.

## Detailed Findings

### Finding 1: Shift Card Duplicate Time Stacking
- **Rule**: R-14 (Feature Cards) & R-16 (Copywriting & Buzzwords)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 184-188)
- **One-line reason**: Displaying a shift title with time range and immediately repeating `08:00 – 17:00 WIB` directly below it creates redundant text noise.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Ensure shift titles are clean names (e.g. `Shift Pagi`, `Shift Sore`, `Shift Malam`) and present the schedule time crisply in a single dedicated time badge or clean mono line.

### Finding 2: Deduction Policy Name Technical Slop & Redundant Data Packing
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting) & R-36 (No Fabricated / Redundant Claims)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (line 203), `prisma/seed.ts` (lines 28, 39)
- **One-line reason**: Storing and displaying policy names as compound parameter strings (`Per Minute Standard (Rp500/min, 10m Grace)`) floods shift cards and table columns with messy text.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Clean policy names to concise titles (`Standar Per Menit`, `Deduksi Per Jam`), update existing database records and seed data, and display clean rate chips on cards (`Rp500/menit`).

### Finding 3: Dense Multi-Row Key-Value Stacking in Shift Cards
- **Rule**: R-14 (Component Craftsmanship) & `antislop-ui`
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 196-209)
- **One-line reason**: Three stacked key-value rows with bold labels and wrapping red text make cards feel like an AI template form rather than a sleek dashboard widget.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Redesign shift card body into sleek, modern micro-metrics: inline badge for grace window (`Toleransi 10m`), rate chip (`Rp500/menit`), and staff indicator (`6 staf aktif`), eliminating row clutter.

### Finding 4: Tutorial-Style Copywriting in Add Shift Modal
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting) & `antislop-copywriting`
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 318-326)
- **One-line reason**: Checkbox label uses a 10-word tutorial sentence (`Shift Lintas Hari (Melewati tengah malam hingga hari kalender berikutnya)`) that reads like instructional filler.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Shorten label to sleek and clear `Shift Lintas Hari (Overnight)`, and streamline field labels (`Toleransi (Menit)`, `Jam Mulai`, `Jam Selesai`).

### Finding 5: Tutorial-Style Select Options in Deduction Policy Modal
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting) & `antislop-copywriting`
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 417-422)
- **One-line reason**: Select options spell out textbook explanations in parentheses (`Bulatkan ke Atas (Jam Penuh Berikutnya)`, `Bulatkan ke Bawah (Abaikan Menit)`).
- **Proposed Remedy**: Clean options to direct professional terms: `Proporsional Riil`, `Bulatkan ke Atas`, `Bulatkan ke Bawah`, `Jam Terdekat`.

### Finding 6: Deduction Policies Table Column Bloat & Redundant Cell Microcopy
- **Rule**: R-31 (Every Decision Must Have a Reason) & R-14
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 236-263)
- **One-line reason**: Displaying `Proporsional Riil` for per-minute mode where rounding is irrelevant adds false precision, and repeating `% upah harian` clutters table cells.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Show a neutral dash (`-`) for rounding when mode is `PER_MINUTE`, and clean column headers and cell values to concise formatting.

### Finding 7: Missing Interactive Management Controls on Shift Cards & Policies
- **Rule**: R-26 (Interactive Elements) & C-2 (Functional Completeness)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 181-210, 250-264)
- **One-line reason**: Shift cards and policy rows can only be created but cannot be edited or deleted, making them feel like a static mockup.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Add subtle action buttons (Edit Shift, Delete Shift / Policy with safety check) to give complete management capabilities.

### Finding 8: Shift Name Double Hours Echo Cascading into Dropdowns and Rosters
- **Rule**: R-02 (Copywriting) & R-16
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (line 470), `src/components/owner/EmployeeAttendanceRoster.tsx`
- **One-line reason**: Employee creation modal appends `({s.startTime}-{s.endTime})` to `s.name`, outputting awkward double bracketed times like `Morning Shift (08:00 - 17:00) (08:00-17:00)`.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Normalize shift display name helper across all views so clean shift names and schedules are formatted consistently without duplicate time expressions.
