Date: 2026-11-20
Target: Payroll & QR Attendance Platform (CyberGuard)
Audit Scope: In-depth visual and copywriting audit of 5 user screenshots: PDF Payroll Modal (/owner/payroll), Recent Activity Widget (/owner), Attendance Trend Chart (DashboardAnalytics), Employee Directory Table (/owner/employees), and Raw Attendance Log Table (/owner/attendance).

## Executive Summary

Following manual review of 5 uploaded screenshots, an audit was conducted identifying specific visual slop, technical enum leakage, repetitive microcopy, and jarring formatting tells across the platform.

Key craftsmanship failures identified:
1. **Shouting ALL-CAPS & Fake Paper Bureaucracy in PDF Modal**: The PDF export modal uses bold shouting uppercase titles (`LAPORAN PENGGAJIAN & REKAPITULASI UPAH`), displays raw checksum hashes, and draws an artificial dotted signature line simulating paper bureaucracy.
2. **False-Alarm Semantic Coloring & Value Repetition**: In the payroll summary table, `Rp0` deduction is rendered in alarming bright red, while `0 hari` and `0 menit` are mechanically repeated on every row.
3. **Filler Activity Feed Exposing Raw Backend Enums**: The dashboard's "Aktivitas Sistem Terkini" displays `AUTH_LOGIN_SUCCESS` with `Aktor: owner (OWNER)` repeated 6 times in a row, offering zero operational utility.
4. **Alarmist Red Block Chart & Metric Duplication**: The attendance trend chart displays solid monolithic red bars of 8 absences across every date without meaningful context, while duplicating 4 KPI cards that already exist on the dashboard.
5. **Inconsistent Status Enums**: The employee directory displays raw database enum `ACTIVE` alongside localized labels `Tetap` and `Kontrak`.
6. **Artificial Truncation & Dense Action Buttons**: Device names are unnecessarily clipped at 140px (`Ahmad's Authorized Pi...`), and dual action buttons are repeated across every row.
7. **English "at" Formatting & Redundant Date Echo**: Raw attendance timestamps print British English prepositions (`10 Nov 2026 at 16:02:26`) inside `JAM MASUK`, duplicating the date already shown in column 1.
8. **Misaligned Status in Time Column**: The string `Sedang Bekerja` is displayed inside the `JAM KELUAR` column rather than using a dedicated attendance status badge.

Below is the prioritized list of 8 actionable findings. Per antislop guidelines (Mode 2), no code files will be modified until you approve the specific numbers to fix.

## Detailed Findings

### Finding 1: Shouting ALL-CAPS & Paper Bureaucracy in PDF Modal
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting - All-Caps Emphasis), R-20 (Visual Identity)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/payroll/page.tsx` (lines 328-415)
- **One-line reason**: The print modal uses uppercase shouting headlines (`LAPORAN PENGGAJIAN & REKAPITULASI UPAH`), displays raw checksum strings (`SHA256-SNAP-...`), and simulates paper bureaucracy with dotted signature lines.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Convert to clean sentence case (`Laporan Rekapitulasi Penggajian & Upah`), remove the pseudo-legal checksum text, and modernize the signoff section into a clean digital verification summary.

### Finding 2: False Alarm Red Coloring & Microcopy Repetition in PDF Table
- **Rule**: R-01 (Color & Semantic Tint), R-14 (Component Craftsmanship)
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/payroll/page.tsx` (lines 389-393)
- **One-line reason**: Coloring `Rp0` deduction in bright red creates false panic, while repeating `0 hari` and `0 menit` on every single row clutters the financial sheet.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Show neutral text or a dash (`–`) for zero deductions, and show clean figures without repetitive units on every cell (`0`, `–`).

### Finding 3: Raw Action Enums Flooding the Recent Activity Widget
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting & Buzzwords), antislop-ui (Filler Activity Feed)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/page.tsx` (lines 233-247)
- **One-line reason**: Displaying 6 consecutive rows of `AUTH_LOGIN_SUCCESS` with `Aktor: owner (OWNER)` exposes internal technical enums instead of human-meaningful activity.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Translate action enums into human Indonesian (`Login Pemilik`, `Pembaruan Shift`, `Verifikasi Presensi`), deduplicate consecutive auth events, or display meaningful operational events.

### Finding 4: Alarmist Red Block Chart & Metric Duplication
- **Rule**: R-05 (Page Structure), antislop-ui (Charts Without a Question)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/components/owner/DashboardAnalytics.tsx` (lines 184-260)
- **One-line reason**: The bar chart fills every day with solid red blocks of 8 absences, creating visual panic, while rendering 4 KPI cards that duplicate existing dashboard metrics.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Remove the duplicate 4 KPI cards so only the primary dashboard metrics exist; adjust chart calculations to only plot active scheduled shifts, or switch to an insightful attendance distribution visualization.

### Finding 5: Inconsistent Language Enums in Employee Status
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting), R-31 (Every Decision Must Have a Reason)
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (lines 294-300)
- **One-line reason**: When employee status is not `PERMANENT` or `CONTRACT`, it falls back to rendering raw database enum `ACTIVE` in English, causing language fragmentation.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Normalize status mapping in database and UI so all statuses are consistently displayed in Indonesian (`Aktif`, `Tetap`, `Kontrak`).

### Finding 6: Artificial Text Truncation & Repetitive Action Buttons
- **Rule**: R-14 (Component Craftsmanship), R-15 (Call to Action)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (lines 332-365)
- **One-line reason**: Truncating device names at 140px on wide screens produces ugly ellipses, while repeating dual bordered buttons on every row adds visual noise.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Allow device names natural room to render without premature clipping; streamline row actions into a compact, elegant action menu or subtle secondary action triggers.

### Finding 7: English Preposition "at" & Redundant Date Echo in Raw Logs
- **Rule**: R-02 (Copywriting / Natural Language), R-14 (Table Column Design)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/attendance/page.tsx` (lines 235-241)
- **One-line reason**: Using `en-GB` formatting prints British English preposition `at` (`10 Nov 2026 at 16:02:26`), and repeats the date already stated in `TANGGAL SHIFT`.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Format time columns strictly as clean time (`16:02:26 WIB`), eliminating the redundant date and English `at`.

### Finding 8: Misaligned Column Data in Raw Attendance Logs
- **Rule**: R-14 (Component Craftsmanship), R-20 (Visual Identity)
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/attendance/page.tsx` (lines 238-244)
- **One-line reason**: Placing the text `Sedang Bekerja` inside the `JAM KELUAR` column violates column semantics and alignment.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Display a dash (`–`) for uncompleted checkout in `JAM KELUAR`, and reflect the ongoing work status in the status column with a clean badge.
