Standard: Anti Slop Rules for AI Coding Agents (R-01 to R-40), antislop-ui, antislop-copywriting
Mode: Mode 2 (After Audit)

## Executive Summary

Following a manual user review of the Employee directory (`/owner/employees`) and associated dashboard views, an in-depth audit was performed focusing specifically on **"too much texto"** (excessive text density, verbose subtitle padding, multi-stacked cell labels, and heavy visual controls).

The audit identified key areas where the interface feels cluttered and machine-generated rather than crafted and sleek:
1. **Multi-Stacked Microcopy Clutter**: Every single table cell stacks multiple lines of redundant text (e.g., employee code + email, shift name + duplicate schedule hours, attendance count + badge + subtitle).
2. **Dense Action Button Clutter**: Displaying two full colored action buttons (`Atur Gaji` and `Reset Perangkat`) on every row creates heavy visual noise across data tables.
3. **Explanatory Subtitle Padding**: Headers and modals feature verbose 15-word AI filler explanations of basic concepts.
4. **Lack of Sleek Data Navigation**: The employee directory lacks instant search and filter controls, dumping dense text rows without breathing room.
5. **Card Label Over-Specification**: Shift and KPI cards use long compound labels (`Toleransi Keterlambatan: 10 menit`, `Batas Maksimal Potongan`) instead of sleek, modern data presentation.

Below is the prioritized list of 12 actionable findings. Per antislop guidelines (Mode 2), no files will be modified until you approve the specific numbers to fix.

## Detailed Findings

### Finding 1: Verbose AI Subtitle Padding on Employee Page
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting & Buzzwords) & `antislop-copywriting`
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (line 192)
- **One-line reason**: A 15-word AI template sentence ("Kelola profil karyawan, tarif upah harian, jadwal shift kerja, dan status binding perangkat.") crowds the top header with empty explanation.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Replace verbose subtitle with sleek stats metadata: `8 Karyawan Terdaftar • 1 Perlu Verifikasi` or keep it to a concise 4-word statement.

### Finding 2: Shift Column Duplicate Time Stacking
- **Rule**: R-14 (Component Craftsmanship) & R-02
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (lines 266-269)
- **One-line reason**: The shift cell prints the shift name (which already includes `16:00 - 00:00`) and then prints `16:00 - 00:00 (Shift Malam)` directly underneath, creating duplicate text clutter.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Clean the shift title to show the shift name on line 1 (e.g. `Shift Malam`) and the clean formatted hours on line 2 (`16:00 – 00:00 WIB`), with zero duplication.

### Finding 3: Heavy Dual-Button Clutter in Every Table Row
- **Rule**: R-15 (Call to Action) & R-14
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (lines 292-315)
- **One-line reason**: Stacking two full-sized colored buttons (`Atur Gaji` and `Reset Perangkat`) on every single row turns the data table into a busy control board.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Streamline actions into a sleek, compact layout: a subtle secondary button for `Atur Gaji` and a clean icon-only button with tooltip for device reset, reducing visual weight.

### Finding 4: Multi-Stacked Subtitles Under Every Table Cell in Attendance Roster
- **Rule**: R-14 (Component Craftsmanship) & R-06 (Typography)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/components/owner/EmployeeAttendanceRoster.tsx` (lines 252-300)
- **One-line reason**: Every column adds a second line of text (`dari 6 shift`, `Shift dilewati`, `0/6`, `Tarif: Rp175.000/hari`), bloating row height and generating dense "too much texto" clutter.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Remove redundant microcopy lines. Show clean standalone numbers with crisp semantic coloring, moving background details to tooltips or detail view.

### Finding 5: Missing Employee Search and Filter Bar
- **Rule**: R-14 (Component Craftsmanship) & `antislop-ui`
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (lines 187-224)
- **One-line reason**: A 10+ row employee table without a search bar or quick filter looks like an unstyled AI prototype rather than a production management tool.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Add a sleek, modern search bar with instant filtering by employee name, code, or shift, plus quick status filter pills.

### Finding 6: Dual-Tagging Overload in Roster Columns
- **Rule**: R-20 (Visual & Verbal Identity) & R-09 (Badges)
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/components/owner/EmployeeAttendanceRoster.tsx` (lines 256, 273, 294)
- **One-line reason**: Rendering both a bold number AND a colored badge pill side-by-side (`0 [Tepat Waktu]`, `5 [Alpa]`) creates badge exhaustion and visual clutter.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Keep badges only for special states or display clean numbers with colored indicators (`0 tepat`, `5 alpa`), eliminating heavy pill badges in every table cell.

### Finding 7: Explanatory Filler in Modals & Redundant Echo
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting) & R-31
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` (lines 333, 455, 483)
- **One-line reason**: Modals include explanatory sentences that state the obvious ("Masukkan data profil...", "Perubahan tarif harian akan tercatat..."), and dynamically re-prints the input number directly below the input box.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Strip filler descriptions from modals; keep form titles crisp and remove the redundant dynamic echo below the salary input.

### Finding 8: Overly Verbose Labels in Shift Cards
- **Rule**: R-02 (Natural Human Language) & `antislop-copywriting`
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 194-205)
- **One-line reason**: Shift cards use bureaucratic multi-word labels (`Toleransi Keterlambatan: 10 menit`, `Kebijakan Denda: Per Minute Standard...`, `Staf Ditugaskan: 6 karyawan`).
- **Proposed Remedy**: Condense to sleek, modern microcopy: `Toleransi: 10 menit`, `Denda: Standar Per Menit`, `Staf: 6 orang`.

### Finding 9: Bloated Table Headers in Deduction Policies
- **Rule**: R-14 (Component Craftsmanship) & R-06
- **Tier**: Quality Lock
- **Priority**: LOW
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/shifts/page.tsx` (lines 225-231)
- **One-line reason**: Headers like `Aturan Pembulatan Jam` and `Batas Maksimal Potongan` are unnecessarily wordy for tabular columns.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Simplify headers to `Pembulatan Jam` and `Batas Maksimal`.

### Finding 10: Explanatory Microcopy Filler Under KPI Cards
- **Rule**: R-16 (Buzzwords & Padding)
- **Tier**: Quality Lock
- **Priority**: LOW
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/payroll/page.tsx` (lines 222, 230, 238)
- **One-line reason**: Stacked lines like `Akumulasi gaji sebelum potongan`, `Penalti keterlambatan terhitung`, and `Nominal siap ditransfer` add visual clutter below KPI numbers.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Remove explanatory filler lines; let clear card titles and prominent monetary figures speak for themselves.

### Finding 11: Repetitive Unit Text in Payroll Table Cells
- **Rule**: R-14 (Component Craftsmanship)
- **Tier**: Quality Lock
- **Priority**: LOW
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/payroll/page.tsx` (lines 250-258)
- **One-line reason**: Headers use wordy titles (`Hari Kerja Selesai`, `Frekuensi Terlambat`, `Total Menit Terlambat`), while rows repetitively print `hari`, `kali`, `menit` in every row.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Shorten headers to `Hari Kerja`, `Terlambat`, `Total Menit`; format numbers cleanly in tabular columns.

### Finding 12: Long Multi-Sentence Subtitles in Security & Audit Pages
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting & Padding)
- **Tier**: Quality Lock
- **Priority**: LOW
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/devices/page.tsx` (line 251), `src/app/owner/audit-logs/page.tsx` (line 166)
- **One-line reason**: Paragraph-length subtitles in device security and audit logs take up valuable screen real estate with tutorial-style explanations.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Condense to single, sleek lines: `Perangkat terdaftar dan persetujuan akses karyawan.` and `Riwayat aktivitas administratif dan log keamanan sistem.`
