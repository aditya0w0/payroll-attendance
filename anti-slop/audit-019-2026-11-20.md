## 2. Issues Discovered and Resolved

- Issue: In `src/lib/attendance-analytics.ts`, an unrecorded day check (`dateHasActivity`) was incorrectly filtering assigned employee shifts in the employee roster calculation, causing assignedShiftCount to drop to 3 instead of 5 when records were absent for scheduled dates. Additionally, `totalScheduledShifts` was being incremented twice during daily aggregation loops.
- Resolution: Removed the artificial `dateHasActivity` condition from employee roster scheduled date checks and eliminated duplicate `totalScheduledShifts` increments in `src/lib/attendance-analytics.ts`.
- Verification: Ran `npx tsx scripts/test-analytics-aggregation.ts`, resulting in 37/37 tests passing.
