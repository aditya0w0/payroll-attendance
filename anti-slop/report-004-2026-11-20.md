## Executive Summary

All 8 approved findings from `anti-slop/audit-004-2026-11-20.md` addressing **"Shift slop writing and too much texto"** have been fully remediated across the Shift management views, Deduction Policies, database seeds, and cascading employee dropdowns.

Key craftsmanship transformations:
1. **Deduction Policy Name Decluttering (R-16, R-36)**:
   - Stripped technical data packing from policy names in `prisma/seed.ts` and `prisma/dev.db`.
   - Replaced clumsy compound titles like `Per Minute Standard (Rp500/min, 10m Grace)` with crisp, professional titles (`Standar Per Menit`, `Deduksi Per Jam`).
2. **Eliminated Time Range Echo on Shift Cards (R-14, R-16)**:
   - Cleaned shift titles to pure names (`Shift Pagi`, `Shift Sore`, `Shift Malam`).
   - Replaced redundant stacked hours with a single, crisp schedule line (`08:00 – 17:00 WIB`) and a subtle `Lintas Hari` badge.
3. **Redesigned Shift Card Microcopy (R-14, antislop-ui)**:
   - Replaced the three stacked key-value rows (`Toleransi:`, `Denda:`, `Staf:`) with sleek inline badge chips:
     - Grace window badge: `Toleransi 10m`
     - Clean deduction rate chip: `Rp500/menit` (with subtle semantic tint)
     - Staff count indicator: `6 staf aktif`
4. **Interactive Shift & Policy Management (R-26, C-2)**:
   - Added complete Edit and Delete capabilities to shift cards and deduction policy table rows.
   - Built backend API endpoints (`PUT /api/owner/shifts`, `DELETE /api/owner/shifts`, `PUT /api/owner/policies`, `DELETE /api/owner/policies`) complete with tamper-evident audit logging (`SHIFT_UPDATED`, `SHIFT_DELETED`, `DEDUCTION_POLICY_UPDATED`, `DEDUCTION_POLICY_DELETED`) and safety guards preventing deletion of shifts with active staff.
5. **Tutorial-Style Modal Copywriting Stripped (R-16, antislop-copywriting)**:
   - Modal 1 (Tambah/Edit Shift): Replaced the 10-word tutorial explanation `Shift Lintas Hari (Melewati tengah malam hingga hari kalender berikutnya)` with concise `Shift Lintas Hari (Overnight)`. Cleaned labels to `Jam Mulai`, `Jam Selesai`, and `Toleransi (Menit)`.
   - Modal 2 (Tambah/Edit Policy): Removed textbook parenthetical definitions from hourly rounding options (`Bulatkan ke Atas`, `Bulatkan ke Bawah`, `Jam Terdekat`, `Proporsional Riil`).
6. **Deduction Policies Table Polished (R-31, R-14)**:
   - Replaced irrelevant `Proporsional Riil` text with a clean neutral dash (`–`) for per-minute policies where rounding does not apply.
   - Simplified maximum limit column formatting to `Maks. 100%`.
7. **Cascading Dropdowns Normalized (R-02, R-16)**:
   - Normalized shift option rendering in `src/app/owner/employees/page.tsx` and `EmployeeAttendanceRoster.tsx` to prevent duplicate time string stacking (`Shift Pagi (08:00 – 17:00)`).
