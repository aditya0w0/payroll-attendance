## Executive Summary

All 8 approved findings from `anti-slop/audit-005-2026-11-20.md` have been fully resolved across the six targeted platform surfaces.

Key craftsmanship transformations:
1. **Landing Page Localization & Honest Navigation (R-16, R-24, C-2)**:
   - Completely rewritten in natural, professional Indonesian (`src/app/page.tsx`).
   - Replaced generic AI claims ("Zero-Trust Architecture", "Mobile-first interface") with clear, factual product descriptions.
   - Fixed false-choice navigation: "Portal Pemilik" and "Portal Karyawan" now route to `/login?role=owner` and `/login?role=employee`, seamlessly preselecting the corresponding tab on the authentication screen.
2. **Terminal QR Operator & Kiosk Features (R-26, antislop-ui)**:
   - Added interactive kiosk controls: a dedicated Fullscreen toggle button and an instant manual QR refresh action.
   - Replaced verbose instructional filler and threat paragraphs with a concise status indicator and clean token rotation bar.
3. **Dashboard Metric Cards Decluttered (R-05, R-16)**:
   - Stripped redundant multi-sentence subtitles beneath KPI numbers (`Karyawan aktif terdaftar`, `X tepat waktu`, `Semua perangkat aman`).
   - Integrated compact inline status badges (`staf aktif`, `X tepat`, `X denda`, `perlu tinjau`).
4. **Security Panel Humanized & Plumbing Hidden (R-20, R-31, C-1)**:
   - Eliminated raw cron syntax (`Scheduled via Vercel Cron (*/10 * * * *)`) and all-caps emphasis badges (`VERIFIED INTACT`).
   - Translated technical developer plumbing into sleek business language: `Integritas Rantai Audit: Rantai Valid`, `Sinkronisasi berkala tiap 10 menit`, and clean cryptographic cards.
5. **Lokasi & Jaringan Cleaned & Standardized (R-16, R-29, R-01)**:
   - Localized header to `Lokasi Kantor & Verifikasi Jaringan` and removed rigid section numbering.
   - Replaced the horizontal sprawl of 9 wrapped city buttons with a sleek dropdown select control (`Pusatkan ke Kota`).
   - Removed hardcoded hex values (`#2C2C2E`, `#3A3A3C`) and raw palette classes in favor of Apple HIG semantic design variables.
6. **Export Rekap & Financial CSV Polished (R-16, antislop-copywriting)**:
   - Replaced all-caps shouting headers in exported CSVs with professional sentence-case Indonesian metadata.
   - Simplified legalistic confirmation dialogues into concise operational prompts.
