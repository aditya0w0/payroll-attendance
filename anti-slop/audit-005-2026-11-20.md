## Executive Summary

A comprehensive multi-page audit was conducted across the six platform surfaces highlighted by the user: Landing Page, Terminal QR, Dashboard, Security Alerts, Lokasi & Jaringan, and Export Rekap.

The audit verified adherence to the 40 Mandatory Rules (R-01 to R-40) and the Craftsmanship Standards (C-1 to C-5), identifying persistent AI slop patterns:
1. **Language Inconsistency & Generic AI Copy**: Landing page and settings headers use English copy (`Office Location & WiFi Verification`, `Zero-Trust Architecture`, `Attendance and Daily Payroll`) while the rest of the application is in Indonesian.
2. **False Choice Navigation**: Landing page displays two distinct cards for "Owner Console" and "Employee Portal", but both point to the exact same `/login` URL without distinction or role pre-selection.
3. **Plumbing & Developer Internals Exposed to Business Users**: The security audit panel displays raw cron expressions (`*/10 * * * *`), technical cryptographic hashes, and all-caps technical badges instead of clean business-ready security indicators.
4. **Instructional Subtitle Padding & Threat Microcopy**: Terminal QR and settings pages stack 15-word tutorial paragraphs explaining obvious concepts (e.g. telling users to place the screen at the office door and warning about legal violations).
5. **Missing Kiosk and Operator Controls**: The Terminal QR screen lacks a dedicated kiosk fullscreen action and immediate manual refresh control essential for front-desk displays.
6. **Hardcoded Colors & Inconsistent Theme Tokens**: The settings page uses raw hex values (`#2C2C2E`, `#3A3A3C`) and inconsistent classes (`bg-blue-600`) rather than semantic CSS design tokens.
7. **All-Caps Emphasis in Export Metadata**: Financial CSV exports and modal dialogues rely on shouting all-caps strings (`"CYBERGUARD FINANCIAL PAYROLL..."`, `"FINALIZED & LOCKED (SNAPSHOT)"`).

Below is the prioritized list of 8 actionable findings. Per antislop guidelines (Mode 2), no code files will be modified until you approve the specific numbers to fix.

## Detailed Findings

### Finding 1: Language Inconsistency & Buzzword Claims on Landing Page
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting & Buzzwords), R-02 (Copywriting), R-36 (No Fabricated Claims)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/page.tsx` (lines 14-75)
- **One-line reason**: The landing page is written entirely in English with generic AI phrases ("Zero-Trust Architecture", "Mobile-first interface") creating a jarring mismatch with the rest of the Indonesian application.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Localize the landing page into crisp, natural Indonesian, align terminology with the platform, and remove empty buzzwords.

### Finding 2: False Choice Navigation on Landing Page
- **Rule**: R-24 (Navigation), R-26 (Interactive Elements), C-2 (Functional Completeness)
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/page.tsx` (lines 38-68)
- **One-line reason**: Presenting two separate cards for "Owner Console" and "Employee Portal" that both route to the same generic `/login` creates an illusion of choice that deceives user expectations.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Provide clear, purposeful actions: either a single primary login CTA with role guidance, or pass role query hints (e.g. `/login?role=owner` / `/login?role=employee`) to preset appropriate login tabs.

### Finding 3: Instructional Microcopy Padding & Missing Kiosk Fullscreen Action on Terminal QR
- **Rule**: R-26 (Interactive Elements), R-16 (Copywriting), antislop-ui
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/qr/page.tsx` (lines 53-112)
- **One-line reason**: Terminal QR features verbose instructional paragraphs and security warnings, but lacks essential kiosk functionality (fullscreen toggle and immediate refresh button).
- **Proposed Remedy**: Add an interactive Fullscreen toggle button and clean refresh button; streamline explanatory subtitles into concise operational status indicators.

### Finding 4: Template Dashboard Metric Subtitle Clutter
- **Rule**: R-05 (Layout & Page Structure), R-16 (Copywriting), antislop-ui
- **Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/page.tsx` (lines 96-147)
- **One-line reason**: Every metric card stacks an extra line of explanatory microcopy beneath the primary number, creating repetitive visual padding without adding actionable insight.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Replace verbose microcopy with clean contextual trends or compact status indicators (e.g. highlight pending requests only when greater than zero).

### Finding 5: Developer Plumbing & Raw Cron Strings Exposed in Security Health Panel
- **Rule**: R-20 (Visual & Verbal Identity), R-31 (Every Decision Must Have a Reason), C-1 (Intentionality)
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/audit-logs/page.tsx` (lines 195-300)
- **One-line reason**: Exposing raw developer infrastructure details like `Scheduled via Vercel Cron (*/10 * * * *)` and public key truncations treats business owners like infrastructure engineers.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Translate technical mechanics into sleek, human business indicators: `Otomatis tiap 10 menit`, `Integritas Rantai: Terverifikasi`, and clean status badges.

### Finding 6: Mixed-Language Header & City Preset Button Clutter in Settings
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting), R-14 (Component Craftsmanship), R-02
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/settings/page.tsx` (lines 218-227, 301-312)
- **One-line reason**: Header uses English `Office Location & WiFi Verification` while body is Indonesian, and 9 city preset buttons wrap awkwardly across the map header.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Change header to `Lokasi Kantor & Verifikasi Jaringan`, replace the 9 raw buttons with a sleek dropdown select or compact pill group, and remove numbered section labels.

### Finding 7: Inconsistent Styling & Hardcoded Color Values in Settings
- **Rule**: R-29 (Color Palette), R-01 (Color), antislop-ui
- **Tier**: Quality Lock
- **Priority**: LOW
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/settings/page.tsx` (lines 234, 284, 290)
- **One-line reason**: Use of hardcoded hex values (`#2C2C2E`, `#3A3A3C`) and arbitrary colors (`bg-blue-600`) breaks theme consistency between dark and light modes.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Replace hardcoded values with semantic Apple HIG CSS variables (`var(--text-primary)`, `var(--system-bg-secondary)`, `var(--accent)`).

### Finding 8: Shouting All-Caps Emphasis in Export Metadata & Wordy Dialogues
- **Rule**: R-16 (Copywriting - All-Caps Emphasis), R-36, antislop-copywriting
- **Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `src/app/owner/payroll/page.tsx` (lines 64, 93-100)
- **One-line reason**: CSV export metadata lines and confirmation dialogs use shouting all-caps text and verbose paragraphs that feel machine-generated.
- **Proposed Remedy**: Convert all-caps metadata to natural sentence case (`Laporan Rekapitulasi Gaji & Presensi`, `Status: Final / Terkunci`) and condense confirmation dialogues.
